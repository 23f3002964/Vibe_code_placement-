About the Placement Portal

The University Placement Portal is designed to bridge the gap between students and potential employers. Our platform facilitates seamless communication and provides tools for both students and companies to achieve their goals.

Our Mission

To connect talented students with leading companies and provide opportunities for professional growth and development.

Our Vision

To become the premier destination for university career services, fostering successful partnerships between academia and industry.

Services We Provide

  • Job posting and application management
  • Career counseling and guidance
  • Workshops and training programs
  • Resume review and interview preparation
  • Industry networking events
